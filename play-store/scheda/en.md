# Google Play listing – English (en-US)

## Title
buonApp – Turin school menus

## Short description
What's for lunch at school today? Turin's school meal menu, day by day.

## Full description
What's for lunch at school today? buonApp tells you in a second: open the app and you'll see today's lunch at your child's school, taken from the menus published by the City of Turin.

WHO IT'S FOR
Parents and families whose children use the school meal service in Turin, Italy: nurseries (6–9, 9–12 and 12–36 months), preschools, primary schools and middle schools.

WHAT IT DOES
• Shows today's menu or, if there's no school today, the next school day.
• Shows the whole week, day by day, with arrows to move to the previous or next week.
• Covers every diet: standard menu, no meat, no meat or fish, gluten-free, no animal protein, no beef, no pork.
• Shows the afternoon snack separately from lunch when the menu includes one.
• Saves up to two favourite menus – for example for two children at different schools – and shows them as soon as you open the app.
• Works offline: it keeps the last menus you viewed on your phone.
• Updates itself when the City publishes or corrects a menu.
• Available in Italian, English, French, Spanish and Arabic. Dish names stay in Italian, exactly as the City publishes them.

SIMPLE AND PRIVACY-FRIENDLY
No sign-up, no ads, no personal data. Favourites stay on your phone only. To know how many people use the app we count visits anonymously, without cookies.

GOOD TO KNOW
buonApp is not an official app of the City of Turin: it is an independent project that reads the menus from the City's public calendars. If in doubt, what the City of Turin and the school communicate prevails. When a menu has not been published yet, the app says so and links to the official calendar.
